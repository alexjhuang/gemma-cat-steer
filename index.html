How it works

The model is a 4-bit ONNX export of Gemma-2-2B-it with a few extra nodes compiled into every decoder layer. Each layer can subtract a direction from the residual stream, and layers 8 and 14 can additionally remove and push negative one Gemma Scope sparse-autoencoder feature. With all inputs zero the graph is the stock model, which is what the left column runs. The features and the direction were found by contrasting cat sentences with matched dog sentences.

The activation views read the residual stream the graph exposes after each layer. The strip is the cat feature's activation on every token; the heatmap is each token's projection on the cat direction at every layer, centred per layer; the candidates are the five most likely next tokens at the first answer step, cat-related ones in red; the stack glows with the cat-direction magnitude at the latest token. The first token (BOS) is an attention sink and is left out.

Outputs are deliberately altered research artefacts. Strong settings produce substitutions and occasional nonsense; that is the point.
