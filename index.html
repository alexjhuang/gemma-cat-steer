How it works

The model is a 4-bit ONNX export of Gemma-2-2B-it with a few extra nodes compiled into every decoder layer. Each layer can subtract a direction from the residual stream, and layers 8 and 14 can additionally remove and push negative one sparse-autoencoder feature (Gemma Scope, 65k width). The features and the direction were found by contrasting cat sentences with matched dog sentences. With all inputs zero the graph is the stock model, which is what the left column runs. Full method, numbers and caveats in the report.

Outputs are deliberately altered research artefacts. Strong settings produce substitutions ("Dog") and occasional nonsense; that is the point.
